# Statue credits

The four statue models were generated on 29 October 2026 with Tencent's open-source
[Hunyuan3D-2 mini](https://github.com/Tencent/Hunyuan3D-2) image-to-3D model (Tencent Hunyuan
Community License) from one photograph each, then cleaned up and reduced to 60,000 triangles. The
game paints each one a single finish (bronze, marble or sindoor). They're interpretations of the
statues in the photos, not scans.
